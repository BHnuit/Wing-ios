Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Welcome to ZenWing. We understand how important privacy is to you, which is why a "Privacy-First" approach is our core design philosophy. This Privacy Policy explains how ZenWing handles your data.

1. 100% Local Storage

All your journal entries, images, audio recordings, and settings are stored completely locally on your device (via iOS's secure database). We have no backend servers to store your journal data, and the developer has no way to access, view, or collect your private records.

2. Secure Key Storage (Keychain)

ZenWing allows you to use third-party AI models for journal synthesis. All API Keys you provide are securely encrypted and stored using Apple's highest-grade security mechanism, the Keychain. This information is never uploaded to any server or shared with other third parties.

3. Third-Party AI Services & Data Sharing

When you use AI functionalities such as "Journal Synthesis" or "Memory Extraction", certain data is transmitted over an encrypted network to the third-party Large Language Model provider of your choice.

3.1 Data Types Shared

The following types of data may be sent to the AI provider:

  • Text fragments: The text content you enter in the chat interface as daily records.
  • Image descriptions: Text descriptions associated with images you attach (images themselves are not sent).
  • Long-term memory content (if enabled): Semantic, episodic, and procedural memories extracted from your previous journals.

3.2 How Data Is Collected

All data shared with AI services is voluntarily provided by you through the app's chat interface. ZenWing does not automatically collect or transmit any data without your active input and explicit consent.

3.3 User Consent

ZenWing requires your explicit consent before sharing any data with third-party AI services:

  • A clear consent dialog is presented before any AI feature is used for the first time, explaining exactly what data will be shared and with whom.
  • You can revoke your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data → Revoke Data Sharing Consent.
  • If consent is revoked, AI features will not send any data until you re-authorize.

3.4 Supported AI Providers

Data is sent directly from your device to one of the following providers, depending on your selection:

  • Google Gemini — Terms of Service
  • OpenAI — Privacy Policy
  • DeepSeek — Privacy Policy

ZenWing does not operate any intermediate servers. Your data transmission only happens between your device and the destination provider. We strongly recommend reviewing the official privacy policy of your chosen provider.

4. Data Collection and Tracking

ZenWing does not contain any hidden tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or user behavior analytics tools. We do not collect your usage habits for commercial or advertising purposes. All your interactions within the app are private and entirely under your control.

5. Your Rights

Because all data is stored on your local device:

  • Modification and Deletion: You can modify or permanently delete any journal content within the app at any time. This irreversibly erases the corresponding data from your device.
  • Data Export: You can export and back up all your data in standard formats via the in-app "Data Management" feature, giving you full ownership of your data at all times.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Relevant updates will be published on this page. If there are significant changes, we will notify you within the app.
